Safety first

Designed so nothing gets missed.

Every boarding and drop-off is recorded against the stop where it happened. If a child is still marked on the bus when a trip ends, the app asks before it lets the trip close.

  • Per-stop attendance trail
  • Trip can't close with children unaccounted for
  • Parents notified by push, not just in-app
